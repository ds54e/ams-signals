# AMS Signals

AMS Signals helps engineers explore public analog and mixed-signal (AMS) verification, circuit and behavioral modeling, and AI-assisted analog circuit design activity, alongside independent catalogs for Analog, Digital and Lab projects.

[Visit AMS Signals](https://ams-signals.com/).

## Reading the site

- **[Home](https://ams-signals.com/)** shows Activity Trend, recent Events and recently updated projects. The chart covers twelve months; each line scales to its own peak at 100%, so percentages compare trends within a series rather than counts across series. The current month is partial.
- **[Timeline](https://ams-signals.com/timeline/)** lets you explore dated Company and People trajectories. Use Search and the Company filter, then select an Event to inspect its evidence.
- **[Events](https://ams-signals.com/events/)** provides the complete chronological record within the selected Golden scope, focused on company-related developments in AMS verification, analog/behavioral modeling and AI-assisted analog circuit design. Search or filter by Signal type, open an Event's stable link and follow its public sources. Company and Person pages provide additional context.
- **[Analog](https://ams-signals.com/analog/)**, **[Digital](https://ams-signals.com/digital/)** and **[Lab](https://ams-signals.com/lab/)** list projects with descriptions, primary links, functional Scope and reviewed public activity. Use Search and the Scope filter to find projects, then follow Website, Paper, Code or Results links where available.
- **GitHub** in the site navigation opens this repository.

Scope uses plain functional labels such as Design or Verification. Project descriptions explain where AI is used in the workflow. A separate **AI-ASSISTED** badge credits evidenced AI contributions to building the software; select it to see the explanation and sources. See the [provenance policy](docs/AI_DEVELOPMENT.md).

Public evidence establishes what was disclosed, not company-wide maturity or private capability. Missing public activity does not prove inactivity. See the [research boundaries](PROJECT_CONTEXT.md#bounded-source-grounded-research) for how evidence is interpreted.

The Golden Event, Company and People records and their sources are available through the site pages and the repository data. The Analog, Digital and Lab catalogs are separate from that corpus. [Repository documentation](docs/README.md) includes contributor setup and the detailed contracts.

# AMS Signals

AMS Signals helps engineers explore public analog and mixed-signal (AMS) verification, circuit and behavioral modeling, and AI-assisted analog circuit design activity, alongside independent catalogs of Analog and Digital design projects.

[Visit AMS Signals](https://ams-signals.com/).

## Reading the site

- **[Home](https://ams-signals.com/)** shows Activity Trend, recent Events and recently updated projects. The chart covers twelve months; each line scales to its own peak at 100%, so percentages compare trends within a series rather than counts across series. The current month is partial.
- **[Timeline](https://ams-signals.com/timeline/)** lets you explore dated Company and People trajectories. Use Search and the Company filter, then select an Event to inspect its evidence.
- **[Events](https://ams-signals.com/events/)** provides the complete chronological factual record, covering AMS verification, analog/behavioral modeling and AI-assisted analog circuit design. Search or filter by Signal type, open an Event's stable link and follow its public sources. Company and Person pages provide additional context.
- **[Analog](https://ams-signals.com/analog/)** and **[Digital](https://ams-signals.com/digital/)** list projects with descriptions, primary links, design-stage Scope and reviewed public activity. Use Search and the Scope filter to find projects, then follow Website, Paper, Code or Results links where available.
- **GitHub** in the site navigation opens this repository.

Catalog development-provenance badges describe publicly evidenced AI contributions to building the software. Select a badge to read its explanation and sources. These labels are separate from AI used in a project's design workflow; the [provenance policy](docs/AI_DEVELOPMENT.md) explains the criteria.

Public evidence establishes what was disclosed, not company-wide maturity or private capability. Missing public activity does not prove inactivity. See the [research boundaries](PROJECT_CONTEXT.md#bounded-source-grounded-research) for how evidence is interpreted.

The [factual export](https://ams-signals.com/export.json) contains the Golden Event, Company and People records and their sources. The Analog and Digital catalogs are separate from that corpus and export. [Repository documentation](docs/README.md) includes contributor setup and the detailed contracts.
